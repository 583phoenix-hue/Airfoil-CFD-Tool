Drag a point up or down to change the target pressure coefficient (Cp) at that position. Release to update. Suction (negative Cp) points up, matching the Cp plots elsewhere in AeroLab. The ringed points at the leading and trailing edge are shared between both surfaces (they're the same physical point) — dragging one moves both. Check Symmetrical to link every point (shown in purple) for a zero-camber design, without needing to match each side by hand. The curve starts from the seed airfoil's own pressure distribution at the chosen Reynolds number and angle of attack; Reset curve goes back to it.
